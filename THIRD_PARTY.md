## Design reference

[Brendan Bycroft's LLM Visualization](https://bbycroft.net/llm) inspired the synchronized narration and architecture exploration. No reference code, fonts, images or logos were copied. Generated design compositions were used during planning and are not served by the application. The functional diagrams are original HTML/SVG UI generated from the Atlas's curated content.
